Businesses are described by industry, not by name — figures are pulled directly from live campaign reporting.

Problem

The funnels were technically running, but a Meta delivery-setting change quietly throttled spend mid-campaign without anyone noticing for over two weeks.

Approach

Audited spend pacing daily, caught the Cost Per Goal misconfiguration, corrected delivery settings, and rebuilt the funnel-to-high-ticket pipeline across 5 offers.

Result

₹23,26,518 revenue on ₹1,73,988 ad spend — 13.37x ROAS and 461% ROI, including 33 high-ticket conversions worth ₹20,17,000.

Problem

Needed to scale app installs across two platforms without knowing which one was actually cost-efficient at scale.

Approach

Ran Meta App Promotion for brand awareness alongside Google UAC for volume, tracking blended CPI monthly and shifting budget toward whichever platform was winning.

Result

28,315 installs across 8 months at a blended ₹6.80 CPI — Google UAC delivered installs at roughly 3x lower cost than Meta throughout.

Problem

Ads and Business Profile were running as two disconnected efforts — searchers found the clinic, but tracking and local visibility weren't reinforcing each other.

Approach

Search + Performance Max campaigns against high-intent terms ("dermatologist near me," "acne treatment near me"), paired with a Business Profile optimization pass tracked against the same booking calendar.

Result

232 conversions at ₹214.39 cost/conversion, and Business Profile interactions up 61.8% after optimization — including a 67.4% jump in direction requests.

Problem

Needed a ₹499 entry offer that could reliably feed a ₹5,000 premium upsell without cost per sale creeping up as spend scaled.

Approach

Built the entry-course funnel with WhatsApp and email nurture into the premium upgrade, then iterated targeting and creative across six sequential launches.

Result

141 course sales, 15 premium upgrades (10.64% upsell rate), and CPA improved from ₹268 to ₹153 across six campaigns — a 43% reduction.

Problem

Ad spend wasn't translating into enough qualified inbound leads for the sales team to work, and cost per lead was climbing with impressions.

Approach

Restructured campaigns around Lead Generation objectives, tightened placements to Feed + Reels, and pruned underperforming audiences monthly.

Result

806 leads generated across 7 months on ₹1,14,143.93 spend, holding average CPL to ₹114.09 at an 11.5% lead rate on link clicks.

Problem

The brand wanted to move conversations to WhatsApp cheaply and reliably, but early campaigns were spending inefficiently on cold audiences.

Approach

Ran Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns split by launch phase, then scaled the best-performing ad set once cost per lead proved out.

Result

2,329 WhatsApp leads generated at an average ₹10.94 per lead — scaled cost as low as ₹9.51 in the best-performing campaign.
